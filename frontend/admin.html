Bulk import

Download CSV template

Upload a UTF-8 CSV with the columns expression_id, category, english, telugu, gondi_text, variety, script, audio_filename (optional: transliteration, usage_note, speaker) and a ZIP of the audio files named in audio_filename. Nothing is saved until you confirm the preview. Every new entry starts as a draft.

Without a ZIP, entries are imported as text only and recordings are added later. Update mode lets you re-import a revised file: existing entries take the new values, blank cells never erase what is already there (for example expert-entered Gondi text), and changed text on a reviewed entry sends it back for review.
